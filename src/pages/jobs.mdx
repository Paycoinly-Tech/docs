# Affiliate Product Marketing Specialist

🌎 100% Remote, Worldwide


Paycoinly (https://paycoinly.xyz) is a payment gateway built for the next generation of digital payments. We enable businesses to accept stablecoins, native tokens, tokenized real-world assets, and long-tail crypto, with multi-currency pricing and instant settlement.

We're looking for Affiliate Marketers around the world to help us expand crypto payment adoption in local communities.

As an Affiliate Marketer, you'll introduce Paycoinly to businesses in your area or nearby locations using your unique referral link. Your goal is simple: connect businesses that could benefit from accepting crypto payments with our platform.

We welcome referrals across all types of businesses, including both online and physical/POS businesses. You don't need to focus on a specific industry - we're business-agnostic.

## Responsibilities

* Identify businesses in your local area or nearby markets that may be interested in accepting crypto payments.

* Introduce Paycoinly to businesses across any industry, whether online or physical/POS.

* Help businesses discover a modern payment solution for crypto and digital assets.
